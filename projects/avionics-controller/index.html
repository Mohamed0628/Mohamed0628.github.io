Engineering work

Protected power distribution

Specified a 12–24 V input stage with protected 5 V and 3.3 V supply rails and rail monitoring.

Sensor integration

Planned dual IMUs and a barometer connected to an STM32 microcontroller over SPI and I²C.

Communications and debug

Defined external CAN 2.0 communications, SPI flash for logging, and SWD for development and debugging.

Current scope

Concept-stage architecture; the controller is not presented as fabricated or flight-qualified hardware.

Tools & methods STM32 · CAN · SPI · I²C · Power regulation · Sensor interfaces
