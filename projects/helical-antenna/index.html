PROJECT 06 / RF · Electromagnetics

Design & simulation

3.5 GHz helical antenna

An axial-mode helical antenna designed and simulated near 3.5 GHz for circularly polarized wireless operation.

ANSYS HFSSS-parametersFar-field analysis
Center frequency
≈3.5 GHz
Sweep range
2.5–4.0 GHz

Solver

The context

From design decisions to system behavior.

The project examines how helix pitch and turn count influence impedance matching, bandwidth, and radiation behavior.

My contributions

01

Parametric design

Designed the antenna in ANSYS HFSS and swept helix pitch and number of turns to compare candidate geometries.

02

RF evaluation

Analyzed S11, VSWR, input impedance, radiation patterns, directivity, and realized gain over approximately 2.5–4.0 GHz.

03

Optimization

Compared return-loss and far-field results to optimize matching, gain, bandwidth, and radiation behavior. Optimized configurations achieved approximately 14 dBi simulated peak gain.

Project scope & evidence

Results are from electromagnetic simulation. The approximately 14 dBi peak gain is a simulated result, not a physical antenna measurement.
