My work spans the analog front end, the PCB, the embedded code, and the control loop. I’m interested in how those pieces come together in medical devices, robotics, power conversion, and wireless systems.

I’m completing my B.S. in Electrical Engineering at the University of Minnesota–Twin Cities, with graduation expected in May 2027. Alongside leading hardware at STRIVE Medical, I’m collaborating with clinicians and engineers on early-stage portable neural-recording research.
