Power & controls

100 W synchronous buck converter

A 100 W synchronous buck converter designed around a discrete MOSFET power stage and an STM32G4 digital voltage-control architecture.

Design & simulation

Input
24 V
Output
12 V
Design power
100 W
Controller
STM32G4

Overview

The project connects converter modeling to practical circuit and PCB design: derive the plant dynamics, design the PI loop, compare the models against switching simulations, and plan the power and sensing layout.

My contributions

Switching power stage

Designed a 24 V-to-12 V converter using N-channel MOSFETs, gate-drive circuitry, voltage and current sensing, and protection hardware.

Modeling and loop design

Developed averaged and small-signal models, derived the control-to-output transfer function, and designed a PI voltage loop using Bode analysis, crossover frequency, and phase margin.

Simulation

Used MATLAB and LTspice to compare analytical predictions with switching-stage behavior, component operation, and transient response.

Hardware architecture

Designed a 4-layer power PCB with attention to switching loops, gate-drive paths, grounding, and separation of power and control circuitry. Developed an STM32G4 architecture using PWM and ADC feedback.

System overview +
  1. 24 V input

  3. LC filter
  4. 12 V output

Feedback: voltage sensing → STM32G4 PI controller → PWM / gate driver

Current scope

Design, modeling, and simulation ahead of hardware validation. The 100 W rating is a design target.

Tools & methods STM32G4 · LTspice · MATLAB · 4-layer PCB
