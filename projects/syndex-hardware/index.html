Engineering work

Battery charging and power path

Designed a USB-C-powered BQ24074 Li-ion charging and power-path schematic with input-current configuration, battery NTC monitoring, and system-power distribution.

Regulated power architecture

Developed a 3.3 V TPS63001 buck-boost supply architecture for ESP32-S3 and IMU logic, with haptic drivers supplied through separate upstream branches subject to actuator voltage limits and power-budget verification.

Sensing and optical tracking

Integrated BNO086 IMU and red LED optical marker requirements for stereo camera tracking, alongside ESP32-based communications.

Haptic driver trade studies

Evaluated LRA and piezoelectric actuation, simultaneous three-channel loading, and DRV2667 piezo-driver integration; assessed battery-voltage headroom and high-voltage piezo safety considerations.

Current scope

Schematic design and component selection are in progress. Final PCB validation, haptic performance, and battery-runtime results have not yet been established.
