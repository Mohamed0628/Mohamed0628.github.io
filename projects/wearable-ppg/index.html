Overview

Optical PPG sensing starts with a small photodiode current. The design connects that analog measurement to deterministic sampling and digital processing, while accounting for the motion captured by an IMU.

My contributions

Analog signal chain

Designed a biased photodiode transimpedance amplifier, signal-conditioning circuitry, and analog filtering before a 24-bit ADC.

Mixed-signal integration

Architected a custom PCBA integrating the optical front end, ADC, IMU, ESP32-S3, BLE, and regulated power, with attention to grounding and low-noise routing.

Real-time acquisition

Developed ESP-IDF 5.2.5 / FreeRTOS firmware for ADS131M02 SPI mode 1 acquisition, using DRDY interrupts, 12-byte status/channel/CRC frames, and synchronized IMU measurements. Target acquisition is 1,200 samples/s.

Embedded processing

Implemented a 101-tap, 15 Hz Q23 FIR pipeline to decimate 1,200 samples/s to 120 samples/s, alongside six-channel, eight-tap IMU-referenced NLMS motion-artifact processing. Developed host-side tests for FIR and ADC behavior; motion-rejection performance remains under evaluation.

Current scope

Mixed-signal circuit and PCB development, ADS131M02 acquisition firmware, and DSP implementation. A separate optical characterization board is being designed to evaluate LED–photodiode geometry and front-end behavior. Eight-hour battery life and motion robustness are design objectives, not validated performance claims.
