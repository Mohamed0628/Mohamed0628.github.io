The context

From design decisions to system behavior.

These experiments explore how feedback changes speed and position response, and how model accuracy and actuator constraints influence controller behavior.

My contributions

01

Motor modeling

Modeled DC motor dynamics and developed MATLAB / Simulink models for feedback-control analysis.

02

Speed and position control

Designed and tuned PI speed controllers and PID position controllers, evaluating stability, transient response, and robustness with a Quanser DC Motor Control Trainer.

03

Constraints and nonlinear systems

Investigated integral windup under actuator constraints, applied encoder feedback, and performed system identification and feedback-control development for a nonlinear magnetic-levitation plant.

Project scope & evidence

The work combines modeling with laboratory evaluation. It includes controller tuning and system identification without claiming unreported settling-time or tracking-error measurements.
