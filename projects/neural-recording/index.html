The context

Defining the system before the prototype.

The research focuses on how to acquire small neural voltage signals from implanted electrodes while balancing signal integrity, portability, battery life, storage, and data transfer.

My contributions

01

Clinical and engineering collaboration

Contributing an electrical-engineering perspective to a development effort involving University of Minnesota clinical and engineering stakeholders.

02

Analog front-end research

Investigating low-noise amplification, signal conditioning, and electrode interfaces for small neural signals.

03

Portable system requirements

Evaluating battery life, onboard storage, wireless transfer, and the hardware requirements for limiting noise and motion-related interference.

Project scope & evidence

This project is in early-stage architecture and technology research, before prototype development. It is presented as research, with no claim of clinical use or validated patient-recording performance.
